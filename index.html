Method

Feature dates are working display intervals based on documented events or operating periods. They are not a complete record of when each rail switch existed. Features may be represented as building-centered points or schematic lines until control-point registration is complete.

Overlay independent dated sources, record each source footprint, and classify new intersections as candidates. A crossing is not necessarily a rail junction; a documentary relationship is not a physical track. Record contradictory evidence as well as supporting evidence.
