Reading the map: The historical plat imagery is the primary evidence for building footprints, tracks, and streets. Use comparison opacity to superimpose two survey dates. The optional railroad vector is sourced from Rochester municipal GIS and is not dated to 1942; it is a geometry reference only. No hypothetical radial connections are drawn. Kodak points are approximate site references, not building footprints. Historical subway stations and connections are indexed without inferring physical connections to nearby facilities. The atlas is not yet an exhaustive digitization of all buildings, sidings, or railway routes. The Building 82 marker is a mapped research-tower location, not a confirmed nuclear bunker footprint. New subway drawings are included below as primary-source references; they have not been warped onto the basemap because control-point alignment is not yet established.

Building 82: mapped location versus underground facility

Kodak property brochure identifies Buildings 82A–D at 1999 Lake Avenue; mapped research tower coordinate is 43.20361, −77.62917. Contemporary reporting places the neutron facility below Building 82. The underground chamber's coordinates are not established.
